# SkillBridge: app recording checklist

Record these parts of the actual app in this order. These are screen actions, not narration.

## Before recording

- Run `pnpm dev` from the project folder and open the URL printed in the terminal.
- Sign in with your demo account before filming.
- Rehearse the quiz submission and check that recommendations and resources load.
- Record the browser window with notifications off and text large enough to read.

## 1. Student preferences

**Open:** Your starting point (`/onboarding`).

**Show:** Interests, learning goal, and experience level.

**Do:**

1. Select **Saving** and **Budgeting**.
2. Enter a sample goal: **I want to plan a spending budget and build emergency savings.**
3. Choose **Beginner**.
4. Pause on the completed form, then click **Save and continue**.

## 2. Knowledge check

**Open:** Knowledge check (`/assessment`).

**Show:** One question, answer selection, and question navigation.

**Do:**

1. Click **Start knowledge check**, or **Take the assessment again** if a result already exists.
2. Select an answer and click **Next**.
3. Click **Previous** briefly to show that the answer stays selected.
4. Complete all ten questions. Trim or speed up most of the answering in the video.
5. Show the **Submit assessment** click and wait for the result.

## 3. Saved results

**Show:** Overall score and the **Performance by topic** table.

**Do:**

1. Pause on the score.
2. Scroll to show budgeting, saving, credit, and investing together.
3. Point to one topic where more practice may help, using the actual result.
4. Click **Continue to recommendations**.

## 4. Personalized recommendations - spend the most time here

**Open:** For you (`/recommendations`).

**Show:** How suggestions work, the ranked resources, and one complete explanation.

**Do:**

1. Pause on **How these suggestions work**, including the 45% / 35% / 20% weights.
2. Scroll to the ranked cards.
3. Stop on one card and point to its title, topic, difficulty, provider, ranking score, and explanation.
4. Keep the explanation readable for a few seconds.
5. Open its resource link, briefly show the destination, then return to the app.

## 5. Resource library

**Open:** Explore resources (`/resources`).

**Show:** Search and combined filters working.

**Do:**

1. Show the resource count and cards.
2. Search for a word from a visible title or description.
3. Select a **Topic** and **Difficulty** combination you checked beforehand that returns results.
4. Optionally add **Resource type**.
5. Pause on the filtered results.
6. Click **Clear Filters** to restore the library.

## 6. Saved progress - short closing clip

**Do:**

1. Click **Knowledge check** to return to the saved result.
2. Refresh the completed-results page to show that the result persists.
3. Click **Your starting point** to show that the preferences are saved too.
4. Finish on **For you**, with the recommendations visible.

## Optional: show recommendations changing after a retake

Include this if you want to demonstrate the adaptive behavior.

1. Capture the current result AND its recommendations before retaking the quiz.
2. Keep the same preferences.
3. Click **Knowledge check**, then **Take the assessment again**.
4. Complete and submit another attempt.
5. Capture the new result and click **Continue to recommendations** to capture the new ranking.
6. Place the two result/list pairs next to each other or cut between them with **Before** and **After** labels.

Capture the first state before the retake because the app normally displays the latest completed result. Use the actual scores, topic breakdowns, and ranking returned by the app. A higher total score alone does not guarantee that the ranking changes.

## Short version if time is tight

**Preferences -> one quiz question + submission -> results -> one recommendation explanation -> library search and filters.**

Trim typing, most quiz answering, and long loading pauses. Keep the results and recommendation explanations on screen long enough to read.
